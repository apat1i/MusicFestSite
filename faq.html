FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

The festival takes place at Havneparken Open Arena, Trondheim, Norway. Gates open 1 hour before the first set each day.

The general age limit is 18+. Guests aged 15-17 may enter until 21:00 only when accompanied by a legal guardian over 25.

Tickets are non-refundable unless the event is canceled. You can transfer your ticket name up to 48 hours before the festival starts.

Outside food and drinks, glass bottles, professional cameras, drones, and sharp objects are not allowed inside the festival area.

Multi-day and All Fest tickets are exchanged for a personal wristband at the entrance. Re-entry is allowed until 22:00 each evening.
